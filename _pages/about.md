I am a Research Scientist at ByteDance. My work focuses on image and video diffusion models and their applications across different domains.

I received my PhD from [The University of Hong Kong](https://www.hku.hk/), where I was supervised by Prof. [Yizhou Yu](https://i.cs.hku.hk/~yzyu/) and Prof. [Ping Luo](https://www.cs.hku.hk/index.php/people/academic-staff/pluo). During my PhD, I was a member of [HKU MMLab](https://mmlab-hku.com/), led by Prof. Ping Luo.

At ByteDance, I have contributed to the Seedream series (2.0, 3.0, and 4.0) and the Seedance series (1.0, 1.5, 2.0, and 2.5).

My research interests span computer vision and machine learning, including:

- Region-Level Visual Understanding and Spatial Reasoning
- Image and Video Generative Models
- One-Step and Few-Step Diffusion Models
- Knowledge Distillation

If you are interested in my research or potential collaboration, please feel free to [email me](mailto:qsguo@cs.hku.hk).
